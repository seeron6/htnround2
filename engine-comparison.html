How Punching Face built it

  1. Extract video frames and recover camera positions.
  2. Fit a full-head template to facial landmarks and photographed silhouettes.
  3. Project and blend photos into a texture atlas; estimate unseen surfaces.
  4. Add separate hair detail, glasses and a Newton physics cage.

Check likeness, texture seams, ear shape, hair silhouette and estimated neck / rear regions.

How Meshy built it

  1. Select the front, two side views and a far-side view from the same scan.
  2. Send those cropped images to Meshy’s multi-image-to-3D service.
  3. Generate geometry and textures, then remesh toward 30,000 triangles.
  4. Download the textured GLB. Our import path adds preview deformation.

Check whether the generated face preserves your proportions, glasses and hairstyle. This build does not include our Newton cage.
